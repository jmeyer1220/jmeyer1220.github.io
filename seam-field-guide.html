Diagnostic Field Guide v0.1
Seam
How to find where meaning is degrading in your organization
01 — The claim
Organizations rarely break
all at once.
Meaning leaks first.
The problem usually isn't intent. It's what happens as intent passes through systems, teams, tools, approvals, and handoffs under pressure.
02 — The unit of analysis
Look at the seams —
the moments where one person, team,
or system must interpret what another meant.
→ strategy to execution
→ leadership to operations
→ brand to website
→ product to onboarding
→ policy to behavior
→ diagnosis to experience
03 — Three forces to look for
Force What it feels like
Trust Compression Legitimacy gap "We're legitimate, but we don't look or feel like it."
Signal Loss Meaning drift "Somehow what we meant keeps changing during execution."
Complexity Drag Carrying capacity "Everything technically exists, but nothing moves cleanly anymore."
04 — The diagnostic questions
Apply these at any seam in the organization.
  • ◦ Where does interpretation authority change hands?
  • ◦ Where do people most often say "that's not what we meant"?
  • ◦ Which system is carrying meaning it was never designed to carry?
  • ◦ What has become dependent on institutional memory?
  • ◦ Which seams require the most translation?
  • ◦ What promise does the system make that the experience fails to keep?
05 — The reframe
Branding is not just communication.
It's the system responsible for helping
meaning survive contact with reality.
The question isn't whether your organization has intent.
The question is whether your systems can carry it intact.
