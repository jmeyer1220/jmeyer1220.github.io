Case study

Sightline lived out

This portfolio site isn't just a place to show work. It's a governed artifact — built the same way I build for clients: name the symptom, name the force, lock the decision, ship the page.

01 — Symptom

The brand launched.
The site works.
The systems exist.
Something still feels disconnected.

The failure rarely starts where it becomes visible.

A site can look finished while the real decisions live only in memory.

Typography picked ad hoc. CTAs patched section by section. Category colors that run out before the third tag. Visitors feel the drift even when they can't name it.

Meaning leaks first.

BDR-0005

02 — Force

Where the pressure shows up here

The site teaches a diagnostic framework — but the design choices weren't locked in a traceable system.

Signal Loss — cross-cutting with governance

"Something keeps slipping between strategy and execution. I can't tell where the disconnect is."

Intent lived in conversation. Execution lived in CSS. Nothing held the two together — so every tweak risked becoming a new local rule.

Signal Loss names that seam. The fix isn't more polish. It's governed provenance — written decisions that say what code is allowed to change, and what was ruled out.

BDR-0004

03 — Decision

BDR-governed choices on this site

Symptom → force → decision → artifact. The decision is the step most sites skip.

What is a BDR?

A Brand Decision Record is a short, locked write-up of what we chose, why we chose it, and what we ruled out — so the site doesn't slowly drift away from intent.

  • What — the decision, in one line
  • Why — the problem it solves
  • Ruled out — what we didn't do (kept in repo files, not on this page)

The pages below are artifacts — live proof that a locked BDR became real code.

  • BDR-0009

    Typography migration to Instrument Sans body and Instrument Serif accent — editorial voice without flattening UI metadata.

  • BDR-0004 · BDR-0007

    Three-force IA with Work before About — evidence and taxonomy precede bio and credentials.

  • BDR-0006

    Self-diagnosis inquiry (A/B/C/D) instead of a generic contact form — diagnostic frame through conversion.

  • ADR-007

    Consolidated stylesheet — field guide and homepage share tokens in one style.css, scoped by body class.

  • BDR-0010 · BDR-0011

    Public design-system catalog and this case-study page — footer discovery, one-line provenance on public surfaces.

  • BDR-0017 · BDR-0018

    Layered plain-language readability on the field guide and this page — diagnostic voice without jargon walls.

Debt stays visible: no homepage case study tagged Complexity Drag yet (BDR-0012). Locked BDRs record reality — they don't pretend the site is finished.
