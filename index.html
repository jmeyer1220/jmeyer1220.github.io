Sightline — Intent, intact.

The promise

Boone Supported Living residents could submit maintenance requests online when something needed repair at their residence.

The silence

"The Maintenance Request form had been silently failing for months. A missing API key. No error. No alert. Just requests disappearing into a hole that looked like a floor — from people who sometimes needed urgent help."

The exposure

The read didn't stop at the broken form — it mapped wider delivery risk. Interim fix: Google Sheet sync so staff could see submissions. Diagnosis: custom plugin Mailscope to trace mail from form plugin through WordPress core.

That's what Sightline is for. Organizations rarely break all at once — meaning leaks first, usually somewhere between what was decided and what got built. I find where.
